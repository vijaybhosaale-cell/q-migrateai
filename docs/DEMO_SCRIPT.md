# Demo script (about 8 minutes, one laptop)

**Before the audience arrives**

1. Stop the app if it runs, double-click `reset_demo_data.bat`, then `start_demo.bat`. Wait until the browser shows the
   login page (first start after a reset: about 30 seconds while the models train).
2. Zoom the browser to 90 % on a projector. Close other tabs.
3. Keep this page open on a phone or on paper.

Suggested split: **Vijay** opens and runs steps 1, 2, 5 and the close (PQC and security), **Mereena** runs steps 3 and the
AI models page, **Abdul Fathah** runs step 4, the report and the deployment slide (cloud and data).

---

### 0. Opening (30 s) · login page

> "Quantum computers will break RSA and elliptic-curve cryptography. NIST published the replacements in 2024
> (ML-KEM, ML-DSA, SLH-DSA) and plans to disallow RSA and ECC after 2035. A company has thousands of keys across
> AWS, Azure and GCP. The hard part is knowing **what to migrate first**. That is what Q-MigrateAI does."

- Click the **i** button: "We only read cryptographic metadata, read-only, stored in the EU, export or delete at any time."
- Click the **ciso** chip under *Demo Finance Co.* → **Log in**.
- Say once: "These are two fictional companies generated by our simulator."

### 1. Dashboard (45 s)

- Readiness dial **36 %**. "399 cryptographic assets, **347 quantum-exposed**, only 8 % quantum-safe, 45 Critical."
- Point at *Migrate first*: "The top items are shared KMS keys: one key protects 150 other assets."

### 2. Map dependencies (1 min 30) · the wow moment

- Open **Map dependencies**. Click the big node **AWS-KMS-004** (or pick it in *Most depended-on assets*).
- "Everything in red depends on this one RSA key. If it falls, all of these fall: that is **inherited exposure**,
  which a per-asset scanner misses."
- Click **Migrate Wave 1 live** → confirm.
- While it animates: "Twenty assets move to hybrid X25519 + ML-KEM-768 or ML-DSA. Watch the counters:
  quantum-exposed goes from **347 to 236**, coverage from 8 % to 29 %, Critical from 45 to 17."

### 3. Assess risk (1 min) · AI explanation

- Open **Assess risk**, click any Critical asset.
- "Each score comes with reasons in plain words, the formula breakdown, and what the neural network relied on."
- Optional: open **AI models**: "Five models compared on held-out data with cross-validation. The neural network with
  graph features scores best on macro-F1, and we show the confusion matrix. Honest note: the labels are synthetic."

### 4. Plan waves (1 min) · what-if

- Open **Plan waves**. Drag the **what-if slider** to 30: "If we migrate only the top 30, exposure drops to about
  195 and Critical to 2, for about 165 person-days. This is how a CISO argues for budget."

### 5. PQC lab (1 min 30) · validate, migrate, roll back

- Click **Lab** on the first row of the queue (a certificate authority).
- Show the targets with real key and signature sizes from FIPS 203/204/205.
- **Run validation checks** → walk through PASS / WARN. "These are policy checks; a live handshake test is next."
- **Migrate** → then **Roll back**: "Crypto-agility: every change keeps the previous policy, one click undoes it, and it
  is written to the audit log."

### 6. Measure and report (45 s)

- Open **Measure and report**: before/after, NIST readiness now about 68 %.
- Click **Download PDF report** and open it: "One click, ready for management."

### 7. Security and multi-tenancy (45 s)

- Open **Audit log**: the wave, the migration and the rollback with user and time.
- Log out, click **admin** under *Demo Health Group*: "A second customer. Different estate, no access to the bank's
  data. In PostgreSQL this is enforced by row-level security, not only by the code."
- Optional: **Settings → Your account → Set up two-factor** shows the QR code.

### 8. Close (30 s)

> "Real today: the platform, AI engine, planning, PQC lab, reports, security, multi-tenancy and the EU deployment.
> Simulated today: the cloud data. Next step: the real AWS connector, with the read-only access already designed."

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Page shows "No inventory yet" | The first discovery is still running; wait 30 s and refresh |
| Wave 1 already migrated from a rehearsal | Map page → **Reset to discovered state**, or run `reset_demo_data.bat` before starting |
| Browser did not open | Type `http://localhost:8002` |
| Black window closed | Double-click `start_demo.bat` again (data is kept) |
| "Too many failed attempts" | Wait 15 minutes, or restart the app (limits are kept in memory) |

## Likely questions

- **Is the data real?** No, synthetic, generated per company profile; real connectors are designed (see REAL_VS_SIMULATED.md).
- **Why hybrid and not pure ML-KEM?** Hybrid keeps classical security if a new PQC scheme has a flaw, and old clients
  still connect; this is the approach used in TLS deployments today.
- **What is new compared with existing tools?** Graph-based inherited exposure + harvest-now-decrypt-later in the risk
  model, explained per asset, and turned directly into waves with validation and rollback.
- **GDPR?** Metadata only, EU hosting, consent, export and deletion built in; the privacy policy is a template for review.
